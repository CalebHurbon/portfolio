About this Show

This show was a challenge because it was a new space for us. The venue was also very old and under-equiped for our needs and cast size.

However, with these limitations came new growth. I had the chance to practice classic theatrical lighting techniques; using gobos and DIY light trees to add deapth and contrast for this production. Due to short staffing, it was nessisary to automate all lighting cues; syncronising them with the audio and the projections. To pull this off, we had to run our own cable from backstage so that the Mac could connect to the over-stage projector, the audio snake, and the light board in the booth.

Aside from the incredible student tech crew, stage manager and followspot operators, I am most proud of my automation that I used to syncronize the show. I learned alot about Qlab this year.

 

Location: The Ritz Theatre, Escondido
Number of cues: 204
Follow Spot Model: Lycian Super Arc 400 HTI
